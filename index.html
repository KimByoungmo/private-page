Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to AI assistants, GPTs, custom actions, web tools, and related services operated by the site owner. It explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when users interact with these services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the functionality being used, we may collect:

  • User prompts and messages
  • Information voluntarily submitted by users
  • Data required to complete API requests
  • Technical and usage information necessary for operation
  • Authentication data when required by third-party services

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is voluntarily provided by the user.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide requested services and functionality
  • Generate responses and analyses
  • Communicate with third-party APIs when requested by the user
  • Maintain security and reliability
  • Monitor performance and troubleshoot issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Services

Some services may connect to third-party APIs, databases, search providers, cloud services, or external platforms. Information required to complete a user request may be transmitted to those services.

Third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to:

  • Provide requested functionality
  • Process action requests
  • Comply with legal requirements
  • Protect security and prevent abuse

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

7. Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Rights

Where applicable, users may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

These services are not intentionally directed toward children under the age required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by the revised date shown above.
